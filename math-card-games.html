Math card games for kids

A single-page game hub where kids practice math by playing cards: Twenty Total and Memory Match, with math-question popups, player accounts, statistics tracking, and a credit shop for unlockables. Written in vanilla JavaScript, no frontend framework, and tested end-to-end with Cypress.

What the app does

Practice math, earn points and credits, unlock cosmetics. A game loop that makes kids want one more round.

Game Hub is an educational gaming site built around two card games: Twenty Total, where players combine cards to hit a total of twenty against the clock, and Memory Match, a classic pair-matching game. Both weave in math question popups, so every round doubles as practice.

Players register an account, with passwords encrypted with crypto-js before they're stored, then earn points and credits as they play. A protected statistics page tracks per-game performance, and the credit shop lets players spend their winnings on unlockable card backs and color themes that persist on their profile.

The whole thing is a framework-free single-page app: Navigo handles client-side routing, hand-rolled components render each page, and a json-server REST API persists users, stats, and products. Nineteen Cypress suites, both end-to-end and component tests, keep it honest.

A player's journey

  • 1Sign up or log in. Accounts are stored through a REST API with crypto-js-encrypted passwords; guests are routed back to login.
  • 2Pick a game in the hub. Twenty Total (3-10 columns) or Memory Match (4-40 cards), both configurable before each round.
  • 3Play against the timer. Card flips, sound effects, and math question popups keep the round moving.
  • 4Score points, earn credits. Results post to the player's account and roll into their statistics.
  • 5Spend at the credit shop. Unlock card backs and color themes; active picks apply across the games.
